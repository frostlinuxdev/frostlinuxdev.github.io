Duet Night Abyss runs on Linux with no tweaks needed. You can install it through [Heroic Games Launcher](https://flathub.org/en/apps/com.heroicgameslauncher.hgl) with the PC installer or through the Epic Games Store.

## What you need

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Heroic Games Launcher** | [Lutris](https://flathub.org/en/apps/net.lutris.Lutris), Bottles or Steam also work |
| **[ProtonPlus](https://flathub.org/en/apps/com.vysp3r.ProtonPlus)** | Recommended; gives you Proton-GE, CachyOS and more |
| **Installer (.exe)** or an **Epic Games account** | From the [official site](https://duetnightabyss.com/) or the Epic Store |

## Method 1: PC installer in Heroic

1. Download the PC `.exe` from the [official website](https://duetnightabyss.com/).
2. In Heroic press **Add Game**, type the name and select a runner such as Proton-CachyOS latest (Proton-GE also works).
3. Press **Run installer first**, select the `.exe`, agree and install.
4. Close the installer, then press the folder icon next to **Select executable** and choose the launcher in `drive_c/Program Files (x86)/Duet Night Abyss`.
5. Press **Finish**, play to open the launcher and install the game.

## Method 2: Epic Games Store

1. In Heroic go to **Stores > Epic Store** and sign in.
2. Search for the game, add it to your library and press **Download**.
3. Leave the defaults, optionally change the Wine runner, install and play.

The video used no tweaks and logged in on the EU server.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need Windows to play this on Linux?**  
No. The game runs through Proton or Wine, a compatibility layer for Windows games.

**The game does not start. What should I try first?**  
Update your GPU drivers and Vulkan packages, try the newest Proton-GE, and create a fresh prefix if the old one may be corrupted.
